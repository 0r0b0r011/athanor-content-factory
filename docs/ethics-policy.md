# Ethics Policy

## Non-negotiable constraints

Every piece of content produced by the Athanor Factory must satisfy ALL of the following:

### 1. Learning accuracy

- The educational concept must be factually correct
- Misconceptions must be named, not reinforced
- If the creator is unsure about the concept, research must precede production
- No decorative concepts: the learning goal must be causally essential to the narrative

### 2. Developmental appropriateness

- Language complexity must match the declared age band
- Cognitive load must be within age-appropriate limits
- No content may shame, humiliate, or frighten the learner
- Emotional stakes must be proportionate and age-safe
- Conflict must resolve through competence, not fear or trauma

### 3. No manipulation

- No dark patterns (urgency, FOMO, loot-box mechanics, manipulative rewards)
- No fear-based retention strategies
- No emotional exploitation disguised as "engagement"
- Personalization must be transparent, not deceptive

### 4. Safety

- No overstimulation (pace, contrast, sound effects, screen cuts)
- No content that requires a parent to say "that's not how it works"
- Adult trust must be preserved: a parent or teacher watching alongside must not feel betrayed
- AI-generated visuals must pass a tone/safety check before inclusion

### 5. Social ethics

- No social mechanics that create hierarchy, exclusion, or humiliation
- Cooperation over competition
- Representation must be intentional and authentic, not token
- Content must not assume access (internet, devices, language, literacy)

### 6. Transparency

- AI-generated content must be labeled as such where it matters (images, voice, video)
- The system does NOT impersonate a human creator
- Parent/teacher-facing materials must disclose what the AI did and what a human reviewed

## Release blocks

The QA gate MUST block release if ANY of the following is true:

- The educational concept is decorative (removing it doesn't change the content)
- A factual error exists in the learning content
- The content shames or frightens a child
- Conflict resolution depends on fear, not understanding
- Social mechanics create winners and losers
- Visual/audio production is overstimulating for the age band
- An adult watching alongside would be uncomfortable

## Feedback loop

If any of these conditions are flagged in feedback, the content is immediately pulled and the genome is patched before re-release.
